2. CINEMATIC INTRO EXPERIENCE
=============================

The website opening experience is the most important feature.

Create a cinematic computer-assembly intro.

When the website first loads, show a dark, premium technology-style background.

Do NOT immediately show the normal website.

First show an ENTER EXPERIENCE button so that browser audio restrictions are respected.

Button:
"ENTER SAHANA TECHNOLOGIES"

When the visitor clicks it:

Start the animation and sound together.

ANIMATION SEQUENCE:

STEP 1 — PC COMPONENTS ARRIVE

Computer components should appear from different directions and smoothly move toward the center.

Components can include:

* PC case
* Motherboard
* CPU
* CPU cooler
* RAM sticks
* GPU
* NVMe SSD
* Power supply
* Cooling fans
* Cables

Make the movement look like the components are assembling themselves.

Use smooth easing, realistic positioning, glow and subtle motion blur effects where appropriate.

STEP 2 — PC ASSEMBLY

The components should visually come together and form a complete desktop system unit.

The animation should feel premium and cinematic rather than like simple HTML boxes moving around.

STEP 3 — COOLING FAN

After assembly, cooling fans start spinning.

Add a subtle cooling-fan sound effect.

The fan sound should gradually increase rather than suddenly becoming loud.

STEP 4 — POWER ON

Show a subtle power-on animation.

Add:

* power LED effect
* RGB glow
* subtle electronic startup sound
* screen/light activation

Do not make the sound too loud.

STEP 5 — MONITOR CONNECTION

Animate a cable visually connecting the system unit to the monitor.

Then turn the monitor on.

STEP 6 — SHOP BRAND

The monitor screen should display:

SAHANA TECHNOLOGIES

with a professional subtitle such as:

COMPUTER SOLUTIONS

Use a premium technology animation for the logo/text.

STEP 7 — ENTER THE MONITOR

After the brand animation, smoothly zoom the camera into the monitor screen.

The monitor screen should expand until it becomes the main website.

The transition should feel like the visitor is entering the computer screen.

Then reveal the main website.
