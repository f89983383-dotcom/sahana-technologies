    Who we are

    ABOUT SAHANA TECHNOLOGIES

    Sahana Technologies is a Sri Lankan computer technology business focused on practical, honest guidance about hardware. We work with computer parts, PC components, full PC building, upgrades, repairs and software setup — and we take time to explain why a particular part suits your work, study or gaming.

    Buying a computer can be confusing: many similar-looking parts, wide price differences and constant new releases. Our goal is to make that clearer. This website lets you explore components, understand what each one does, compare approximate local prices and try configurations before you commit.

    Whether you need a simple office machine, a student laptop setup, a gaming rig or a workstation for content creation, we help you understand the trade-offs between performance, budget and future upgrade options.
